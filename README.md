# SCOUT Website

Official SCOUT landing page.

## Brand Direction

SCOUT's core positioning is:

**Springboard. Opportunity. Impact.**

SCOUT is a non-profit education and research initiative committed to empowering students,
professionals, and organisations with practical, future-ready digital and analytical skills
through hands-on learning and capacity-building programmes.

## Website Sections

- About SCOUT
- Learning & Opportunity Ecosystem
- Programmes & Pathways
- Skills & Tools
- Cohort 1.0
- Why SCOUT?
- SDG 4 & SDG 8
- Contact
